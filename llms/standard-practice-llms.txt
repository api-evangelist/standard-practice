# Standard Practice

> Voice AI for healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM) teams. Standard Practice's AI agents place and handle outbound phone calls to insurance payers to automate administrative work — claim status follow-up, benefits verification, prior authorization, and EDI enrollment — so billing teams can scale collections and get paid more, faster.
